## Source
- Institution / portal URL: International Development Research Centre (IDRC / CRDI, Canada). Funding opportunities portal: https://idrc-crdi.ca/en/funding (also `/fr/financement`, `/es/financiamiento`). Drupal site (`<meta name="generator" content="Drupal 8">`). Applications themselves happen on an external platform (SurveyMonkey Apply, `idrc-crdi.smapply.io`), which was not probed.
- Access type (web): web portal (public, no login) plus a public RSS 2.0 feed of the same content type (`/en/funding/rss.xml`). No public API found: `/jsonapi` answers 404. Browser used for all probes: local Chromium fallback (`OBSCURA_REQUIRED=0`), because Obscura (`obscura.sh`) is not available on this machine yet; so none of this evidence was gathered through Obscura, and behaviour behind the stealth routing is not verified. About 10 page loads in total (one repeat of `/en/funding` and of `robots.txt`), spaced at least 5 s apart (crawl-delay). Probe helper: `ingestions/idrc/probes/fetch_html.py` (imports `scrapagents.stealth`).
- robots.txt and Terms of Service: **robots.txt** (read 2026-11-09, https://idrc-crdi.ca/robots.txt, 200): the `User-agent: *` group ends with `crawl-delay: 5`. It disallows `/admin/`, `/search/` (and `/*/search/`), `/user/*`, `/comment/reply/`, `/node/add/`, `/filter/tips`, `/media/oembed`, `/core/`, `/profiles/`, `/*/researcher-name/`, `/*/emr-countries/` and some Drupal README/config files. `/en/funding`, `/en/funding/<slug>`, `/en/funding/rss.xml` and `/en/rss-feeds` are **not** disallowed. Only `Dubbotbot` (disallow `/<lang>/search/`) and `TurnitinBot` (disallow `/`) have their own groups. No sitemap line. **Terms of use** (read 2026-11-09, linked from the site footer / `About IDRC > Policies and commitments`: https://idrc-crdi.ca/en/about-idrc/policies-and-commitments/terms-use; French equivalent https://idrc-crdi.ca/fr/droits-dauteur). It says: "By using this website, you agree to be bound by these terms of use"; governed by the laws of Ontario and Canada; IDRC may change the terms. Copyright: "Unless otherwise stated, the copyright to material on this website is held by the International Development Research Centre (IDRC)." "IDRC permits reading, downloading, copying, redistributing, printing, linking, and searching, for non-commercial or academic purposes, of any of its content, provided that credit and reference is given to IDRC and the original source page." "Users must request and receive, at IDRC's sole discretion, written permission from IDRC prior to making any commercial use of IDRC content on this website" (request with the complete URL and a description of intended use; the French page gives info@crdi.ca as contact); "Users may not make commercial use of such content prior to receiving written permission." Content may be freely reproduced for private, personal use. Content whose copyright is not held by IDRC (e.g. stock photography) stays under its owner's rights. The terms say nothing explicit about robots, scrapers or automated access (no clause prohibits or permits it; "searching" and "downloading" are listed as permitted for non-commercial/academic use). Materials are "not guaranteed to be correct, up to date, or complete". Related pages found by search, not reviewed in full: Open Data Statement of Principles (https://idrc-crdi.ca/en/about-idrc/policies-and-commitments/open-data-statement-principles, about research data, CC-BY style licence) and the Open Access Policy; neither was verified to apply to funding call pages. The separate IDRC Digital Library (`idl-bnc-idrc.dspacedirect.org`) has its own End User Agreement that bans systematic retrieval without written permission; it is a different host and is not part of this source.

## Structure found
- Endpoints or DOM entry points: (1) Listing `GET /en/funding` (server-rendered HTML, Drupal Views embedded as paragraphs; content is in the initial HTML, no XHR needed). Two views on the page, separated by `<h2>Open calls</h2>` and `<h2>Closed calls</h2>`; each row is `div.views-row` with `.views-field-title a[href]` (relative link to `/en/funding/<slug>`), `.views-field-field-award-call-for .field-content` (e.g. Applications, Proposals, Expressions of interest, Letters of interest, Concept notes) and `.views-field-field-award-deadline time[datetime]` (ISO UTC, e.g. `2026-12-01T04:59:00Z`). (2) Detail page `GET /en/funding/<slug>` (HTML). Header fields are Drupal field classes: `field--name-field-award-status` (Open/Closed), `-field-award-call-for`, `-field-award-deadline`, `-field-program`, `-field-award-duration`, `-field-topic`, `-field-award-funded-by`, `-field-award-budget`, `-field-award-poc` (contact email), `-field-funding-awards-type` (e.g. Student award), then body paragraphs (Scope, Eligibility, More details). Detail pages link out to an HTML "detailed call" page (e.g. `/en/idrc-invites-applications-john-g-bene-fellowship-2026`), an FAQ page, a supervisor-letter template PDF (`www.idrc.ca/sites/default/files/sp/Documents EN/...pdf`) and the external application site (SurveyMonkey Apply `idrc-crdi.smapply.io`, not probed). (3) Feed `GET /en/funding/rss.xml` (RSS 2.0, `application/rss+xml`), listed at `/en/rss-feeds` as "IDRC Funding RSS" together with feeds for news-events, perspectives, projects, publications, ria and stories. Each `<item>` has `title`, `link`, `guid` (Drupal node id, e.g. `147196 at https://idrc-crdi.ca`), `pubDate`, `dc:creator` (an IDRC staff email) and `description` containing the full rendered page HTML (deadline, status, budget, scope). Observed 10 items, versus 11 calls in the listing: the oldest listed call (Women's Voice and Leadership Learning Partnership: Global Learning Hub) is not in the feed. (4) Language alternates via `<link rel="alternate" hreflang>` (`/fr/financement/...`, `/es/financiamiento/...`). (5) Email alert form (Drupal webform, antibot-protected) on the page; it is a subscription form, not a data source, and was not used. Not available: `/jsonapi` (404); no CSV/open-data export, sitemap line or API found. `/en/search/...` is disallowed by robots.txt, so the project search (`/en/search/content-type/project`) was not requested.
- Pagination and ordering: No pagination: `/en/funding?page=1` returned the same 11 rows as `/en/funding` (canonical URL unchanged, no pager element in the HTML). All calls (1 open + 10 closed on 2026-11-09) are on one page. Ordering is not strictly by deadline: open call first, then the closed calls in a mostly descending deadline order with exceptions (e.g. 2026-03-04, then 2026-05-01, then 2026-02-26; and 2025-09-18, 2025-08-07, 2025-09-24); it matches the RSS order, which is by `pubDate` descending (post/last-publish date), not by deadline. Open vs closed is the section it appears in; the detail page also carries a Status field.
- Authentication: none. Listing, detail pages, RSS and robots.txt answered 200 to an anonymous browser; no login, cookie wall or token. The only form (funding alert) uses Drupal antibot and is not needed to read content. Submitting an application requires an account on the external SurveyMonkey Apply site (out of scope).
- Payload formats: HTML (listing and detail pages, server-rendered Drupal markup) and RSS 2.0 XML (with HTML embedded in `description`). Attachments linked from detail pages are PDF (e.g. supervisor approval letter template, SurveyMonkey Apply applicant guide); the "detailed call document" seen on the one sampled call is an HTML page, not a PDF. No JSON endpoint found. Payload sizes: listing ~64 KB HTML, detail page ~60 KB HTML, RSS ~150 KB for 10 items. Blocking: no captcha or WAF seen on any probe (all 200, `blocked by: none`), with local Chromium (not Obscura).
- Estimated volume and change rate: Volume is small: 11 funding calls listed on 2026-11-09 (1 open, 10 closed), so about 11 detail pages plus their linked call/FAQ pages. Change rate (from RSS `pubDate`, which is the publish/last-update date of each call): 10 items between 2025-05-29 and 2026-10-16, i.e. roughly 7 new or updated calls per year and a gap of about 4 months (2026-06-22 to 2026-10-16) between the latest two. The newest call (John G. Bene Fellowship 2026) was published 2026-10-16. Closed calls remain visible in the listing (the oldest shown has deadline 2025-06-25); whether IDRC retires older calls from the page was not determined, and the full history is not available from this page. Estimates come from a single day's snapshot and are not a measured trend.
