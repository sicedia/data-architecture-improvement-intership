Phase 2. Written by `source-assessor`. Closed by **Gate H1** (human sign-off in `state.yml`).

## Viability
- Legal / ToS risk: **Medium, with one open question that only a human can decide.** Evidence (`discovery.md`, Terms of use read 2026-11-09, https://idrc-crdi.ca/en/about-idrc/policies-and-commitments/terms-use): the copyright of the site is held by IDRC; IDRC "permits reading, downloading, copying, redistributing, printing, linking, and searching, for **non-commercial or academic purposes**", with credit to IDRC and the original source page; any **commercial use** requires prior written permission, "at IDRC's sole discretion" (request with the complete URL and a description of intended use; info@crdi.ca is the contact on the French page). The terms say nothing about robots, scrapers or automated access: no clause forbids or allows them. robots.txt does not disallow `/en/funding`, `/en/funding/<slug>` or `/en/funding/rss.xml`; it sets `crawl-delay: 5`. Allowed today without a request: automated, rate-limited reading of the public funding pages and the public RSS feed for non-commercial/academic use, keeping attribution (IDRC name + source URL on every record). **Main open question for the human (H1): is Producto OI a non-commercial/academic use, or could it be a commercial use of IDRC content (for example if it is resold, offered as a paid service or exposed to paying third parties)?** This assessment does not decide it. Recommendation: ask IDRC for written permission (info@crdi.ca, with the URLs `/en/funding`, `/en/funding/<slug>`, `/en/funding/rss.xml`, and a short description of Producto OI), keep the answer in this dossier, and do not publish IDRC content outside the platform until the question is settled. Secondary notes: the terms may change at any time and the content is "not guaranteed to be correct, up to date, or complete" (record the retrieval date per record); the Open Data Statement of Principles and the Open Access Policy were not verified to apply to funding call pages, so they cannot be relied on; the IDRC Digital Library (`idl-bnc-idrc.dspacedirect.org`) has a stricter End User Agreement and is out of scope; the external application site (SurveyMonkey Apply, `idrc-crdi.smapply.io`) was not probed and is out of scope; attachments hosted on `www.idrc.ca` fall under the same copyright unless stated otherwise (third-party material such as stock photos stays under its owner's rights).
- Anti-bot risk and mitigation (obscura.sh): **Low as observed, but not verified through Obscura.** All 10 probe page loads (listing, `?page=1`, detail, linked call page, RSS, `/en/rss-feeds`, `robots.txt`, `/jsonapi`) answered 200 (404 for `/jsonapi`) with `blocked by: none`: no captcha, no WAF challenge, no login or cookie wall. The site is server-rendered Drupal 8, so no XHR or JavaScript is needed to read the content. Caveat: every probe used the local Chromium fallback (`OBSCURA_REQUIRED=0`) because Obscura was not reachable; behaviour behind Obscura (different network egress and fingerprint) is unknown. Mitigation: (1) production extraction goes through `scrapagents.stealth.StealthRouter`, i.e. Playwright `connect_over_cdp` to `OBSCURA_CDP_URL`, with `OBSCURA_REQUIRED=1` so a run fails rather than falling back to a local browser; (2) honour `crawl-delay: 5` with at least 5 s between requests, using a **one-slot Airflow Pool** (`idrc_web`) so there is never more than one concurrent request; (3) request volume is tiny, about 13 requests per run (1 RSS, 1 listing, up to ~11 detail pages) i.e. about 1 minute at 5 s spacing, and the RSS-first path needs fewer; (4) respect the robots.txt disallow list (`/search/`, `/*/researcher-name/`, `/*/emr-countries/`, etc.) and never submit the antibot-protected alert form; (5) if a WAF or captcha appears, stop and record it as a finding for H1; do not work around it. Condition for the next phase: re-run `make probe` on the listing, one detail page and the RSS feed once Obscura is reachable, and attach the result to `discovery.md`.
- Personal data (LOPDP): yes. Fields: `field-award-poc` (contact email of the IDRC point of contact, on detail pages), `dc:creator` in each RSS `<item>` (an IDRC staff email) and, inside the HTML embedded in the RSS `description` and in the detail/call pages, possible names, job titles and emails of IDRC staff or programme contacts in the body text. These are work contacts of identifiable people published by IDRC, but they remain personal data under LOPDP, and only one detail page was sampled, so other pages may name individuals not seen. When unsure the answer is yes: a human can downgrade it later. Proposed handling for the blueprint: treat email and person names as personal-data columns (classified in the contract), keep them in Landing/Bronze for lineage only, do not expose them in Staging or Producto OI (or expose only the institutional role/mailbox if one exists), and do not link them to Party. Question for the human: confirm that LOPDP applies to staff of a Canadian institution processed on CEDIA's side and the desired treatment (drop, hash or keep restricted).
- Duplicates an existing CEDIA source?: no. Searched `vendor/cedia/configs/ingestions/` (ces, eu_funding_tenders, idb, openalex, openerp, sia, simpler_grants) and `vendor/cedia/configs/contracts/` (ces, eu_funding_tenders, idb, openalex, openerp, sia, simpler_grants) and `vendor/cedia/docs` for `idrc`, `crdi` and `International Development Research Centre`: no match in configs, contracts or docs, and no source reads `idrc-crdi.ca`. The three funding-like sources are different institutions and endpoints: `eu_funding_tenders` (EU Funding & Tenders Portal, API), `simpler_grants` (US federal Simpler.Grants.gov, API) and `idb` (Inter-American Development Bank open data, files). IDRC is a distinct funder with its own calls, so it would be a new shared source with its own ingestion id, not a second path for an existing dataset. It is complementary to them in domain (funding opportunities), so the later blueprint may align the contract fields with the funding opportunity datasets of `eu_funding_tenders` and `simpler_grants` for a common Intermediate model, but that is a Phase 3 decision. OpenAlex lists funders in works but does not publish IDRC's calls, so it is not an alternative path. IDRC data from another route (IDRC Digital Library, project database) is a different dataset and out of scope.

## Refresh analysis
See `vendor/cedia/docs/ai/data_platform_pre_implementation_gate.md` (every run reads the full scope).
- Full scope per run or incremental: **Full scope on every run** (the platform has no incremental mode, PLAT-016 D1), with growth controlled in bronze by `persistence.mode: changes`. The scope is small: 11 calls on 2026-11-09 (1 open, 10 closed), no pagination, so the whole listing is read every time and tombstones are possible because the read covers the whole scope. **Primary extraction path: the RSS feed `/en/funding/rss.xml`** (RSS 2.0, 10 items, about 150 KB, each `<item>` carries `guid` = Drupal node id, `link`, `pubDate` and the full rendered page HTML in `description` including deadline, status, budget and scope). It is one request, stable (public, listed in `/en/rss-feeds`, not disallowed) and gives a per-record `pubDate` as a publication/last-update signal. Persistence key proposal: the Drupal node id from `guid` (falling back to the detail URL slug); `ignore_fields`: volatile fields such as feed `lastBuildDate`, and any rendered "time since" text, to be confirmed in the blueprint. **The HTML listing `/en/funding` is still needed** for two things the feed does not give: (1) the oldest listed call (Women's Voice and Leadership Learning Partnership: Global Learning Hub), which is listed in the HTML but absent from the 10-item feed; (2) a cross-check of completeness and of the Open/Closed sections (the feed is cut at 10 items and its order is by `pubDate`, not by deadline). So the proposed design is RSS for the records plus a listing read to find calls missing from the feed and fetch their detail page; detail pages (`/en/funding/<slug>`) are read only for calls whose fields are not fully in the RSS `description` (to be checked in the blueprint against the fields listed in `discovery.md`: program, duration, topic, funded by, budget, point of contact, award type). Honest limits of the evidence: (a) the feed is capped at 10 items, so the feed alone will silently drop old calls as new ones appear, which is why the listing must stay in scope; (b) it is not verified that `pubDate` moves when a call closes by deadline (a time-driven status change, which the gate says must be checked), so `pubDate` is not accepted as a change signal, and every run re-reads the full scope and compares payloads in bronze; (c) there is no `ETag`/`Last-Modified` measurement, no sitemap and no history beyond what the listing shows, and it is unknown whether IDRC retires old calls from the page; all estimates come from one day's snapshot, not from two observations. Language scope: English only (`/en/funding`); French and Spanish alternates exist (`/fr/financement`, `/es/financiamiento`) and are out of scope unless the human asks for them.
- Proposed schedule and why: **Weekly full read, for example Monday 06:00 America/Guayaquil (`0 6 * * 1`)**, with the one-slot Pool and 5 s spacing, in production only (`airflow.schedules_enabled`). Why: the change rate is low, about 7 new or updated calls per year (10 items from 2025-05-29 to 2026-10-16, with a gap of about 4 months between the two latest), so a daily run would write nothing to bronze on almost every execution. Weekly is still frequent enough to see a new call within 7 days and, because status closes by deadline passing, to see a call turn closed within a week of its deadline; a full read costs about 1 minute, so the cost is not the limit, the low change rate is. Under `persistence.mode: changes` a run with no changes writes no rows and publishes no Asset. Alternative for the human: `schedule: null` (run on demand), as `simpler_grants`, `idb` and `eu_funding_tenders` do, if no downstream product consumes IDRC data yet; the gate says to leave `schedule: null` only with a stated reason. The cadence is provisional and should be revised after at least a few weekly runs give a measured change rate by segment (open vs closed, by year); do not freeze closed calls, keep them in the weekly full read.

## Recommendation
- Decision (go / no-go): go
- Reason: This is a conditional go: proceed to Blueprint only if the human accepts conditions C1 to C6 below at H1.
- Rationale and conditions: The source is technically easy and low risk: public, no login, server-rendered, a public RSS feed that carries the whole page content, no captcha or WAF seen, tiny volume (about 11 calls, about 13 requests per run) and a low change rate (about 7 calls per year), with no duplicate in CEDIA. The real risks are (1) legal: the Terms allow non-commercial or academic use but require IDRC's written permission for commercial use and are silent about automated access, so whether Producto OI counts as commercial is a human decision; (2) the anti-bot evidence was gathered with local Chromium, not Obscura; (3) personal data (staff emails and names) must be classified and kept out of the product. Conditions: (C1) the human decides the commercial vs non-commercial question and, preferably, written permission is requested from IDRC (info@crdi.ca) and recorded in the dossier; until answered, no IDRC content is exposed outside the platform and attribution (IDRC + source URL + retrieval date) is kept on every record; (C2) re-probe the listing, one detail page and the RSS feed through Obscura (`OBSCURA_CDP_URL`, `OBSCURA_REQUIRED=1`) before Implement; if a block appears, stop and record it; (C3) extraction honours `crawl-delay: 5` with a one-slot Pool and only reads URLs not disallowed by robots.txt; (C4) personal-data fields are classified in the contract and not exposed in Producto OI; (C5) RSS is the primary path and the HTML listing stays as the completeness path for calls not in the feed; (C6) scope is English-only HTML and RSS; PDFs, FR/ES pages and the SurveyMonkey Apply site are out of scope unless the human adds them.

### Questions the human must answer to sign H1
1. **Commercial or non-commercial?** Is Producto OI used for non-commercial/academic purposes only, or could it be a commercial use of IDRC content? Do we ask IDRC for written permission (recommended) and wait for the answer before Implement, or accept the non-commercial reading now?
2. Is automated, rate-limited access (RSS plus HTML, 5 s spacing, one slot) acceptable to the legal owner given that the Terms are silent on robots and scrapers?
3. Is the Obscura re-probe (C2) a blocker for H2 or for Implement?
4. Personal data: confirm that staff emails and names are personal data under LOPDP and choose drop, hash or restricted keep; are IDRC contacts allowed in Producto OI at all?
5. Scope: English only? Include the linked call/FAQ pages and PDF attachments (which would use Tika), or not in the first version?
6. Schedule: weekly (`0 6 * * 1`) or `schedule: null` (on demand) until a consumer product exists?
